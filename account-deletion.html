Delete Your Account

How to delete your Izzy account, and exactly what happens to your data.

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Deletion is permanent

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Your profile, messages, location history, activity stats and ski-buddy connections are removed and cannot be restored — not by you and not by us. If you are the billing parent of a family, your family and the child profiles in it are removed too. Read the rest of this page before you start.

Delete your account from inside the app

This is the fastest route and it takes effect immediately. You do not need to contact us.

  1. Open the Izzy app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom tab bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen.
  4. Tap Delete account — it sits just below Sign Out.
  5. Read the warning on the Delete account? prompt, then tap Continue.
  6. On the second prompt, Are you absolutely sure?, tap Delete my account.

Two confirmations are required on purpose — an accidental tap should not destroy an account. You can back out at either step by tapping Cancel. Once you confirm the second prompt, deletion runs immediately and you are signed out.

Child accounts in a family

A child account added to a family does not have a Delete account button of its own. Child profiles are managed by the parent or guardian who created the family: they can remove an individual child from the family roster in the app, or delete their own account — which removes the whole family, including every child profile in it. See What happens to your family below.

If you no longer have the app installed

You do not need to reinstall Izzy to delete your account. Email solomon@izzywolf.com from the email address on your Izzy account, with the subject "Delete my account".

Sending from the account's own address is how we confirm the request is really yours. If you no longer have access to that address, write to us anyway and we will work out another way to verify you before we delete anything — we will not delete an account on an unverified request, because doing so would let anyone delete someone else's.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 7 days. You will get a confirmation email when it is done.

What is deleted

The following are removed from our systems when your account is deleted:

  • Your account and sign-in identity — your email address and the credentials used to sign in, including a linked Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Your profile — name, date of birth, gender, home and current city/state, skill level, disciplines, tagline, gear colours, voice preference and every other profile field.
  • Your location data — your current and recent locations, and your GPS breadcrumb trails.
  • Your activity data — daily stats and summaries (runs, vertical, speed, distance, duration).
  • Your messages — direct messages you sent and received, group and family conversations, and resort bulletin posts. Because a direct message is a single record shared by both people, deleting your account also removes your side of the conversation from the other person's app.
  • Your connections — ski-buddy friendships in both directions, and any users you had blocked.
  • Your Izzy conversations — transcripts of what you said to the Izzy AI companion, and the facts Izzy had remembered about you.
  • Your safety data — emergency-contact details you entered, check-in requests, family SOS incidents you triggered, and your fall-detection settings.
  • Your devices — registered devices and push-notification tokens, so we stop being able to send you notifications.
  • Your notification preferences and subscription entitlement record.

What happens to your family

Izzy families have one billing parent, and only that person can administer the family.

  • If you are the billing parent: deleting your account deletes the family itself and every child profile in it, including each child's display name and birthdate. This is deliberate — those profiles exist because you consented to them, so withdrawing your account withdraws that consent. Family settings, family conversations and family SOS records go with it.
  • If you are an adult member of someone else's family: only your membership is removed. Their family, and the other people in it, continue unaffected.
  • If a child in your family has their own paired device login, that child's profile data — display name and birthdate — is removed with the family. The child's sign-in identity itself is retired separately rather than automatically, so that deleting one person's account can never silently delete a different person's login. If you want it removed at the same time, say so in an email to solomon@izzywolf.com and we will do it.

What we keep, and why

We would rather tell you precisely what survives than claim a clean sweep we do not perform. None of the items below contain your messages, your location history, your profile or your conversations with Izzy.

  • Photos and videos you uploaded. The messages containing them are deleted, but the underlying image and video files are not removed from storage automatically at this time. If you want them erased as part of your deletion, say so in an email to solomon@izzywolf.com and we will remove them by hand.
  • Safety and moderation records. If another user reported your content or behaviour to us, that report is kept as a safety record. It contains what they reported and an internal account identifier, not your profile.
  • Internal counters. A small number of internal records keyed to your account identifier — anti-abuse rate-limit counters and daily AI-usage totals — are retained. They hold counts and dates only: no name, no email, no location, no content.
  • Your purchase and billing history at the app store. We never hold your card details, and Apple and Google keep their own transaction records under their own policies. We cannot delete those.
  • Anything we are legally required to keep, and backups. Live data is deleted immediately; encrypted operational backups roll off on their own schedule and are not used to restore a deleted account.

Your subscription is separate

Deleting your Izzy account does not cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are billed by the App Store or Google Play, and only you can cancel them, in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings. Cancel the subscription first, then delete the account — otherwise billing continues on a subscription you can no longer use.
