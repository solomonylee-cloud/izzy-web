Safety

Need help? Help knows where you are.

Trigger SOS by voice or tap and Izzy texts your emergency contact and alerts ski patrol with the colors that you're wearing, your live GPS, a Google Maps link, and a breadcrumb trail of your last 60 seconds on the mountain. Take a hard fall and can't reach your phone? Fall detection can trigger it for you.

  • ✓SOS text with location — live GPS, a 60-second breadcrumb trail, and a tappable Maps link.
  • ✓Fall detection — a hard impact can auto-trigger SOS, hands-free.
  • ✓Tap-to-call ski patrol — patrol numbers verified against the resorts’ own sources, and a straight route to 911 where a resort publishes none — no searching when seconds count.
  • ✓Every season — the same SOS, the same fall detection, in Izzy Snow and Izzy Sun.

At partner resorts in our pilot program, patrol receives a real-time alert on their own dashboard the moment you call. See how the loop works →

THE IZZY FAMILY

Inspired for families. Open to all.

Izzy started with one question: how do we keep the whole family safe and connected on the mountain? The Family plan is the heart of the app — it puts Pro in every adult's pocket and gives parents real control over kids on the hill. Set it up once, and the whole family rides covered: 2 adults and up to 4 kids (age 17 and under), in Snow and in Sun.

  • ✓Parent-managed kid accounts — one billing parent sets up the family; each kid gets their own account under a parent's control.
  • ✓Parent-controlled Izzy voice — switch Izzy's voice on or off for each child, any time.
  • ✓Voice or Tap SOS for kids — automatically alerts parents and ski patrol, together.
  • ✓Find-my-family — everyone on a shared family map, live, all day.
  • ✓End-of-day recap — a DM with each kid's max speed, vertical, and runs.

Built for families: riders under 13 join only through a parent-created family account, and a parent controls their settings. See our Children’s Privacy Policy.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Izzy free to try?

Yes. Base is free, with no account required to start: safety SOS and fall detection, 10 minutes of Izzy voice a day, text chat, 5 Izzy Vision snaps a day, and conditions at every resort Izzy covers — in Izzy Snow and Izzy Sun. Pro, Family and Max add minutes, snaps, memory and the family stack on top. See the plans →

How do I talk to Izzy?

Tap Izzy and ask — Izzy answers out loud, then it's quiet until your next tap. Base gives you 10 minutes of Izzy a day; Pro and Family give each adult up to 60, and Max up to 180. No wake word, no menus, gloves stay on.

What is Izzy Sun?

Izzy Sun is summer mode — the same app, the same resorts you already ride, with summer art, a summer map and summer answers: hiking, biking, ziplining, camping, what's open at the lodge. Izzy follows your mountain's calendar, so Sun becomes the default when winter ends and Snow is always one look away. At launch, Sun is a preview until resorts open for summer: you can look around any time from your profile, and live summer conditions arrive when resort activities do. SOS, fall detection and the family map work exactly the same in both.

Which resorts does Izzy work at?

91 mountains across the US and Canada today — the IKON and EPIC resorts, plus a growing set of select independents. Pick yours in the app and Izzy answers with that mountain's lifts, trails, grooming and conditions; SOS and the family map work wherever you ride. See every resort → Not there yet? Ask us and we'll add it.

What is Izzy Vision?

Tap Izzy's eyes on the home screen and the camera lens opens. Snap a trail map, a sign, a lift board or a menu and the photo goes straight to Izzy, who looks at it and talks it through with you in voice. Base includes 5 snaps a day, Pro and Family 25, Max 100. It's photos only (no video), it works in Izzy Snow and Izzy Sun, and it stays off for kid accounts.

What does Izzy know?

Izzy is built for the mountain — live conditions, trail and lift status, grooming, resort info, and where to grab a bite. On Pro and Family, Izzy goes further with live web search, so you can ask about current events, nearby spots, and just about anything beyond the slopes.

How does fall detection work?

Fall detection is included free on every plan. Switch it on in the Safety tab and set the sensitivity yourself — Low, Medium, or High. When it's on, Izzy watches for a hard impact followed by a spell of stillness, then auto-fires your SOS with your last GPS — texting your emergency contact and, at partner resorts, alerting ski patrol — unless you tap "I'm OK" first.

Can I call for help with my voice?

Yes, on Pro and Family — just say "Izzy, I need ski patrol." Izzy texts your emergency contact your live GPS and offers to call patrol, hands on your poles. Base gets a one-tap SOS button.

How does Kid SOS work?

Kids fire SOS from a tap, their voice, or a lock-screen widget. Every parent gets a time-sensitive push, elevated above normal notifications, with the kid's live GPS and a one-tap "Call Patrol" button built right in — parents acknowledge so the kid sees "Mom's on the way," or tap through to reach patrol directly.

What's the difference between Pro and Family?

Pro covers one rider. Family covers up to 6 (2 adults + 4 kids) — full Pro for both adults, kid accounts built for safety — and adds the family stack: live Find My Family map, family chat, Kid SOS, end-of-day recap, and COPPA kid mode for under-13s. Solo rider → Pro; household → Family. See the plans →
