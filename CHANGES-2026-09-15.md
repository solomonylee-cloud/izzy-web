## How resorts.html is normally produced (read from the file, not assumed)

`resorts.html` carries a header comment: "GENERATED by tools/build-resorts.py from the consumer
app catalog — edit the script, not this file." `tools/build-resorts.py` reads
`../SkiBuddyGemini/src/data/resorts.ts` (a sibling repo) and regenerates the whole page,
including the IKON/EPIC/INDY counts, per-region grouping (sorted by full state/province name,
then resort name), and the header/meta counts.

Per the task's explicit scope fence ("do not touch any other repo"), this change **hand-edits the
generated output directly**, matching the exact structure the generator produces (same sort order,
same markup shape, same badge classes). It intentionally diverges from the normal flow — the
consumer catalog in `SkiBuddyGemini` still needs the same 10 additions + Telluride recategorization
made separately in that repo so the generator stays the source of truth going forward. Flagging
this rather than silently leaving the generator out of sync.

`resort.html` is the B2B "Izzy Resort" ops/patrol marketing page — no per-resort names or data in
it, so item 4 of the brief (per-resort page / data file) does not apply. Confirmed by grep: zero
resort names found in resort.html.
